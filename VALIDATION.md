# FrameLinePro 1.2 validation

The signed Release archive, the Debug simulator build and both embedded camera extensions build successfully with the installed Xcode toolchain. The final phone installation uses Release optimization without the Debug diagnostic flag. Minimum deployment remains iOS 26.

## Passed checks

- Connected iPhone: four batches of five HEIF requests, alternating standard/styled processing. All twenty requests completed. Submission was 100 ms apart; total completion includes background rendering and storage, not just shutter timing.
- Connected iPhone: native Bayer RAW capture and development, a draft RAW preview, full-resolution RAW editing, Live Photo movie delivery and depth-map portrait rendering. Test media was not sent to Photos.
- Original bytes unchanged after development; thirty repeated preview edits; a new archive instance restored 250 records and edit recipes, with deletion reflected after saving.
- Viewfinder A/B autofocus measurement, exposure-bias preservation, forward/reverse pulls and twelve cancel/restart cycles. Separate 0.2 → 0.8 hardware focus-position check passed.
- Paired styled video files decoded successfully with matching frame counts and expected 1920×1080 / 1080×1920 dimensions. Camera input/format/zoom remained preserved after finalization.
- Optimized grading compared against full-source reference rendering for three styles, both crops and their left edges. The working image retains enough pixels for either finished output; single-output resolution is unchanged.
- Ten refreshed marketing PNGs verified as opaque RGB, 1320×2868. The fifteen-page tour and looping frosted summary were visually inspected in the simulator. The summary uses a native repeating transform, pauses off-page/background and respects Reduce Motion.

## Limits and remaining manual checks

Sustained styled Dual Shot is still thermally constrained. A short optimized check approached 29 recorded frames/s before the phone became heavily warmed; later serious-thermal-state tests fell well below the requested 30 fps despite producing valid files. The final retained path preserves output dimensions and grading, reduces redundant working pixels and submits both output renders before waiting. **This is not proof of zero heat, zero lag or sustained requested frame rate on every device.** Profiling on cooled devices and older supported hardware is the next performance priority.

Camera-mode tests above validated delivered resources, not every Photos-library presentation path. Confirm paired Live Photo playback and sound in Photos, depth edges around hair/glass, and full-resolution original/edited sharing manually.

Lock Screen control registration, authentication, actual locked capture and session transfer require hands-on device verification. Both extensions compile, sign and install, but that alone does not prove all system interactions. Also check termination during locked capture and gallery restoration after a real force-quit. No public TestFlight/App Store distribution is enabled by this project’s free Personal Team.

## 1.2.1 responsiveness and reliability checks

- Photo editor now retains a decoded preview and renders adjustments directly to a Metal drawable, removing per-slider debounce, file reads and JPEG roundtrips. On the connected phone, 120 changing slider requests produced 120 preview frames with 6.7 ms p95 render completion time. This measures the render path, not end-to-end touch latency. No camera or Photos writes are involved in this diagnostic.
- Depth capture passed three of three attempts with a camera stop/start between attempts. Photo mode no longer probes recording codecs (format changes can disable depth delivery); compatible depth formats and delivery are restored before capture. This verifies delivered portrait rendering on the connected rear camera, not every lens/device or editable Apple Portrait assets.
- Style/preset management passed hide/reorder/restore, custom-style and preset deletion, reset/undo and Codable persistence checks. Standard remains available; deleted custom recipes remain resolvable for existing photo edits.
- Paired video now renders directly to native 8-bit 4:2:0 encoder buffers, with explicit sRGB transfer and Rec.709 matrix/primaries. A reference-image conversion check measured 0.00402 mean linear RGB error. A ten-second phone check produced 305 frames over 10.07 seconds with both expected output sizes and approximately 301 preview frames per view.
- A later 60-second dual-style recording on an already warm phone produced 1663 frames per file over 60.07 seconds (about 27.7 fps), with 1659 preview frames per view and maximum preview gaps of 97/99 ms. Thermal state rose from fair to serious. Both files decoded, and input/format/zoom survived finalization. Sustained requested 30 fps and low temperature remain open performance targets.
- Camera work pauses behind the photo gallery, retained render caches support a smooth return, redundant starts are ignored, and internal last-session restoration no longer shows a loaded-preset toast. Resume input/format/zoom checks passed. Visual cold-start behavior across other supported phones remains a manual check.
- The camera intent is now included in all three targets, requests foreground routing when unlocked, and the secure extension handles hardware camera events as required by LockedCameraCapture. Both extension privacy manifests declare their own-defaults use. The user confirmed that the camera opens while locked after removing and re-adding the control. Locked capture transfer, interrupted capture and the full authentication matrix remain manual checks.

New Debug checks: `--verify-editor-preview`, `--verify-depth`, `--verify-manager`, and `--verify-nv12`. Diagnostic builds use Release optimization with DEBUG enabled; the installed final release must omit that diagnostic override.

- Connected phone: live preview with changing color, exposure, crop and straightening rendered all 120 requested updates, with 23.6 ms p95 render-completion time. - Native input now delivered 1920×3408 8-bit full-range 4:2:0 buffers on this phone. Explicit buffer sizing avoids the previously delivered 2160×3840 RGB analysis stream. Both 1080p crops retain enough source pixels; requested output sizes, grading, codec and frame rate are unchanged.
- One-minute styled dual recording: thermal state stayed nominal (0 → 0); 1683 frames per file over 60.07 seconds, about 28.0 fps. Both previews had 1679 frames, with maximum gaps 108/111 ms. Files decoded and input/format/zoom restoration passed. This test started cooler than the earlier warm-phone test, so it is not a controlled thermal/battery comparison. Sustained 30 fps and reduced battery drain still need broader profiling.
- Final exact-aspect sizing regression: native 1926×3424 input, format 875704422 (full-range NV12), 305 frames per file over 10.10 seconds, both expected output dimensions, 301 preview frames per view, and preserved camera configuration after finalization. The final request uses even dimensions in the exact source aspect ratio rather than independently rounded dimensions; the minute-long measurement above used the preceding 1920×3408 request.

Final hands-on checks: new depth photo → Develop photo → blur/separation → close/reopen; crop placement and straightening with the preview pinned; export/share at 1080/2048/full; Original versus edited image; one-minute styled Dual Shot on a cooled phone; Live Photo, rangefinder and lens transitions after the capture-buffer change. ## 1.2.3 gallery regression and HDR checks — 11 November 2026

- An intermediate stop/restart gallery implementation failed an extended check: one cycle stopped the camera and stalled the UI. That implementation was discarded. The final path keeps the capture session warm and suppresses hidden custom preview/scope processing.
- Connected phone, actual 11-item gallery: twelve real sheet presentations/dismissals, six in Photo and six in Dual Shot Video, including quick 180 ms dismissals and longer visits. All passed. Preview mount count stayed at one, camera remained running, and post-dismissal graded-frame age was 1–35 ms at the check points. Maximum presentation callback latency was 58 ms; the longest 16 ms main-actor heartbeat gap was 129 ms. These measurements rule out the reproduced multi-second stall in this run, not every possible stutter or every supported device.
- Selected deletion of 100 temporary items followed by deleting the remaining 200 passed. An injected failed index save restored the item and kept its file. No user captures or Photos copies were deleted by this test. The combined deletion test took about 0.10 seconds. Photos-import completion is awaited before removing files still needed by that import. Force-termination during pending cleanup remains a manual check.
- HDR pipeline: a known HDR fixture retained headroom 3.48 and decoded peak red 3.47 (above SDR white 1.0) after exposure, square crop, straightening and 1080-pixel export/reopen. The SDR opt-out and HDR-aware gallery thumbnail decode passed. This is a pipeline/metadata/pixel-value check; real-scene style appearance and display brightness still require hands-on review.
- Ninety geometry/export cases, saved-edit/source preservation, recipe/mask reopening and 120 live-preview updates passed again. Latest preview p95 render completion was 25.6 ms in the changing-crop/style test.
- Sustained paired recording with per-frame autorelease pools: 1654 frames per file over 60.03 seconds (about 27.6 fps), two valid 1080p outputs, 1652 preview frames per view and maximum preview gaps 91/93 ms. Thermal state moved from nominal to fair (0 → 1). Camera input/format/zoom restoration passed. This is not an energy benchmark or evidence of sustained 30 fps; thermal/battery work remains open.
- Simulator: selection layout inspected using isolated generated sample photographs. The refreshed feature tour includes adaptive HDR, gallery selection/deletion and a seamless 21-feature summary loop. Final Release must be installed without DEBUG diagnostic flags.

User checks: rapidly open/close Gallery in Photo and Dual Shot, open a detail then return to shooting, select/delete expendable items and verify existing Photos copies remain, inspect Delete all confirmation without committing unless intended, and compare HDR/SDR exports when Preserve HDR highlights appears for an HDR original. Also test gallery return after actually backgrounding the app and on the oldest supported phone.

## 1.2.4 — recording scheduling and resource reuse (11 November 2026)

- Session GPU resources are reused; outstanding GPU/camera work is bounded and buffers retained through completion. Scopes leave the camera delivery queue, RGB buffers are pooled, date-stamp sizes cached, and unchanged storage readings no longer trigger UI updates. Grading functions, spatial effects, crop geometry, output dimensions, codec and bitrate are unchanged.
- A one-minute run produced 1800 frames / 60.07 seconds (29.97 fps), two valid files, preserved camera configuration, thermal nominal → fair. Starting temperatures varied between comparisons, so energy savings are not established.
- Final three-minute phone run: both files read to completion with matching 4743 timestamped samples / 179.997 seconds (**26.35 fps**), 1920×1080 and 1080×1920. Five-second counts fell from 149–150 at the start to 110–113 at the end. Maximum file timestamp gap 68.3 ms. Both previews delivered 4739 frames; maximum steady display gaps 121 ms. Thermal nominal → fair, 656 bounded-queue source drops, camera input/format/zoom preserved. **Sustained 30 fps was not achieved.** No duplicate frames or quality reduction masked this limit.
- The cadence diagnostic initially crashed converting an invalid compressed marker timestamp into an integer bucket. The crash report confirmed the diagnostic-only reader. It now skips nonnumeric timestamps; interruption retest finalized/read both videos and passed recovery. The original long-run files were copied and independently read on macOS with the corrected rule.
- Phone and simulator GPU-order comparisons passed nine frames, three looks, LUT/intensity blends, partial style strength, date stamps, both crops and left edges within one code value of the existing 8-bit resolve. Transparent edges are flattened before difference-blend comparisons to avoid false alpha errors.
- Twelve phone gallery presentation/dismissal cycles passed in Photo and Dual Shot: one preview mount, maximum presentation callback 65 ms, heartbeat gap 117 ms. This gallery was empty; populated-library evidence remains the earlier eleven-item 1.2.3 test. Temporary deletion of 100 selected plus 200 remaining items and failed-index rollback passed in 0.10 seconds. No user media was deleted.
- Slower direct per-crop grading and color-table approximations that exceeded the fidelity limit were rejected and removed.

Remaining: controlled energy profiling, longer loaded scopes/loupe runs, repeated rapid recordings and oldest-supported hardware. Full-resolution grading submission cost remains the next performance target. Sustained 30 fps, zero stutter and reduced battery drain are not established across all devices. Final installation must use normal Release without DEBUG diagnostics.
